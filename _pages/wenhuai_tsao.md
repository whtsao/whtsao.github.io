<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am an Assistant Professor in the <a href="https://www.mcneese.edu/engineering-and-sciences/engineering-and-computer-science/" target="_blank">Department of Engineering and Computer Science</a> at McNeese State University. My vision is to advance resilient and sustainable coastal communities through digital twin technologies that unify high-fidelity, reduced-order, and interactive frameworks for fluid-structure interaction.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I was born in Taipei, Taiwan. I received my Bachelor and Ph.D. degrees in Civil Engineering from National Taiwan University. After completing a post-doc at NTU for one and a half years, I worked at <a href="https://www.caee.utexas.edu/research/research-areas/ocean-engineering" target="_blank">Ocean Engineering Group</a> at UT Austin for two years and at <a href="https://www.lsu.edu/ceds/index.php" target="_blank">Coastal Ecosystem Design Studio</a> at LSU for three years. Then I joined <a href="https://www.cowi.com/north-america/" target="_blank">COWI</a> as a coastal marine engineer. In 2025, I returned to Louisiana to continue my academic journey.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am a licensed Professional Engineer in Texas (No. 157642) and an active member of ASCE, ASME, LES, and SFSS.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I enjoy taking on creative projects. I love sports.
</p>
